# 🎙️ PyBuddy - Voice Assistant  

PyBuddy is a **smart and lightweight voice assistant** built with **Python**. It is designed to understand and respond to voice commands, making everyday tasks faster and easier. From **searching the web** and **checking the time** to **opening apps** and **sending emails**, PyBuddy acts as your **virtual buddy** to automate routine tasks.   

---

## 🚀 Features  
✔️ Voice recognition & natural language processing  
✔️ Open applications and websites quickly  
✔️ Search the web and fetch results  
✔️ Tell the current date & time  
✔️ Send emails via voice commands  
✔️ Play music or videos  
✔️ Lightweight and beginner-friendly  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **Python 3.8+**  
- **SpeechRecognition** – for speech-to-text  
- **pyttsx3** – for text-to-speech  
- **smtplib** – for sending emails  
- **webbrowser** – to open sites  
- **datetime** – for time & date functions  

---
## 📦 Dependencies

- speechrecognition  
- pyttsx3  
- pyaudio  
- requests  
- smtplib (built-in with Python)  
- datetime (built-in with Python)  
- webbrowser (built-in with Python)
